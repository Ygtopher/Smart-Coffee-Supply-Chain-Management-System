# **Smart Coffee Supply Chain Management System**

## **System Overview**

**Smart Coffee Supply Chain Management System** is a comprehensive digital platform designed for IMPEXCOR Ltd to enhance transparency, traceability, and efficiency across coffee supply chain operations. The system digitizes processes from sourcing to distribution, improving batch traceability, inventory management, operational monitoring, and data-driven decision-making in coffee trading and export activities.

---

## **UI Modules & Features**

### **1. User Registration & Authentication Module**

**UI Elements:**

- Role-based registration (Farmer, Aggregator, Processor, Exporter, Quality Controller, Logistics, Admin)
- Secure login with credentials or QR code for field staff
- Multi-factor authentication for sensitive operations
- Profile setup with organization, location, and certification details
- Supply chain role assignment and verification
- Session management with activity logging
- Bulk import for farmer cooperatives

**Features:**

- Secure authentication across supply chain tiers
- Integration with existing business directories
- Role-based data visibility and permissions
- Activity tracking for accountability
- Support for mobile field authentication
